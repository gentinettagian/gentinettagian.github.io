Organised events

As a Ph.D. representative and member of the PolyPhys committee, I had the opportunity to contribute to the organisation of various social events throughout my Ph.D. In addition, I was a member of the organising committee for the following events.

June 2025

ETHZ-EPFL summer school SimMBQP

Summer school on simulating quantum many-body physics using quantum and classical techniques, organised at EPFL for Swiss and international PhD students.

September 2023 and September 2024

NCCR MARVEL junior retreat

Retreats for the 60+ junior researchers of the National Centre of Competence in Research MARVEL, funded by the Swiss National Science Foundation. Students and postdocs had the chance to network, learn, and present in the beautiful setting of St. Moritz (2024) and Davos (2023) with a focus on building bridges between industry and academia.
